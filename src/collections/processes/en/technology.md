## Summary

Accessible technology works with assistive tools, is easy to use, and includes support when challenges or technical issues arise.

## Why is this important?

Technology can help to include many people who would otherwise not be able to participate, for example, people who are not able to travel can join committees remotely or contribute asynchronously. When choosing technologies, it's important to select tools that everyone can use to prevent people from being unintentionally excluded.
