## Summary

Feedback is collected in different ways. The options are accessible, simple to use, and allow enough time so D/deaf and D/disabled people can meaningfully respond.

## Why is this important?

Getting feedback helps reveal gaps in standards and the standards development process. Different perspectives highlight issues that may have otherwise been missed, including the experiences of D/deaf and D/disabled people. A transparent feedback process builds trust by showing that participants’ contributions are valued and considered.
